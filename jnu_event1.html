ABOUT US

Centralized system for streamline event management

Our Vision

Create a unified and transparent platform that connects students, faculty, and organizers through accessible event information.

Our Mission

Simplify event management by enabling organizers to publish details efficiently providing viewers with clear, centralized event information.

JNU Event Adda is a centralized platform designed to manage and showcase academic, cultural, and social events of Jawaharlal Nehru University. It helps students, faculty, and organizers stay informed about upcoming events through a single, easy-to-access system.
